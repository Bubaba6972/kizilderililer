About This Site

Native Americans — The First Peoples of North America is an independent educational website created to introduce the rich history, culture and heritage of the Native peoples of North America.

🎯 Our Mission

The purpose of this site is to tell the thousands-of-years-old story of Native peoples in a way that is accessible, understandable and respectful for everyone. We aim to:

  • Present the origins of Native peoples and their Asian connection based on scientific sources
  • Introduce major tribes such as the Lakota, Navajo, Cherokee, Apache and Iroquois
  • Keep the history of resistance and cultural heritage alive
  • Draw attention to today's struggles for language, land and rights

📚 Our Content

All texts on this site are compiled from open sources (Wikipedia, national park services, academic publications) and written originally. Photos are taken from Wikimedia Commons under free license. The site is published in two languages: Turkish and English.

🙏 A Note of Respect

This site was prepared with respect for the history and culture of Native peoples. If you notice an error or omission in the content, please reach us via our contact page so we can correct it.
